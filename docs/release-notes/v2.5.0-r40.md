# v2.5.0-r40 — RMX mode, a safe headphone cue, and search that stays on the Search page

<!-- debs: rebuild -->

New Mixxx build and new skin files. Install the update as usual.

- **RMX mode: drum pads and rolls.** A new **RMX** button sits under the
  Color FX buttons. Tap it and the waveforms make way for four drum pads:
  **KICK**, **SNARE**, **CLAP** and **HAT**.
  - Tap a pad for one hit. Hold it to roll, in time with the deck that is
    playing, at the **ROLL** rate you pick (1/1 to 1/16 of a beat). Pick
    **1 SHOT** if you never want a roll.
  - **BUILD** plays a two-bar snare roll that speeds up into the drop.
  - **LEVEL** sets how loud the drums are.
  - The top of the panel shows which deck the drums follow, its BPM, and a
    light on every beat.
  - The drums are always in your headphones as well as on the master. To
    practise quietly, turn the master down and play along in the
    headphones.
  - While RMX is open, the BEAT FX buttons on the DDJ-400 play the pads:
    BEAT ◀ kick, BEAT ▶ snare, FX SELECT clap, ON/OFF hat, and SHIFT +
    ON/OFF for BUILD.
  - The LEVEL/DEPTH knob sets the roll rate, so you can hold a pad and turn
    it to speed the roll up. SHIFT + LEVEL/DEPTH sets the drum level.
  - Tap RMX again to get the waveforms back, and the BEAT FX buttons work
    the Beat FX as before.

- **Pressing CUE can no longer blast your ears.** The headphone level was
  raised in r19 so a quiet cue could get loud enough, but that extra gain
  hit everything in the headphones. A loud track, both decks cued at once,
  or the HEADPHONES MIX knob turned towards MASTER could all come through
  far too loud and distorted. Now the extra boost applies only to the
  cued decks, never to the master, and a limiter stops the headphones from
  going past full volume. Quiet tracks still get the full boost.

- **A search only filters the Search page.** Searching for a track used to
  filter Browse as well, so you could not see the rest of the folder. Now
  Browse always shows every track. When you go back to Search your query is
  still there, and it searches whatever folder is selected at that point.

- **Tracks load at their first hot cue.** In r38 a track loaded at its
  earliest cue, which was nearly always the memory cue on the first beat,
  so most tracks still loaded at the start. Now a track with hot cues loads
  at the first of them, and a track without hot cues loads at its first
  memory cue.

## Install (on the Pi)

```
sudo update-pioneered.sh
```

or Cog → **SOFTWARE UPDATE** → UPDATE, twice.

## Field checks

1. Tap **RMX**: the pads replace the waveforms, and the button lights up.
   Tap each pad: you hear the drum in the master and in the headphones, and
   the pad flashes.
2. With a track playing, pick **1/4** and **hold SNARE**: the roll is in
   time with the track. Try **1/16**, then **1 SHOT** (holding a pad now
   plays one hit).
3. Stop both decks and hold a pad: it still rolls, at the last tempo, and
   the header reads **FREE**.
4. Turn the master volume all the way down, with the HEADPHONES MIX knob
   anywhere: the drums are still in the headphones.
5. On the DDJ-400: **BEAT ◀ / BEAT ▶ / FX SELECT / ON/OFF** play kick /
   snare / clap / hat. Hold ON/OFF and turn **LEVEL/DEPTH**: the hat roll
   speeds up and slows down.
6. **SHIFT + ON/OFF** (or tap BUILD): a snare roll starts on the next beat,
   speeds up for two bars and stops right on the drop.
7. Tap **RMX** to close it: the waveforms are back, and BEAT ◀ / ▶ and
   ON/OFF work the Beat FX again.
8. Turn HEADPHONES LEVEL to about 3/4, cue a loud track and **press CUE**:
   it is loud but clean, with no crackle.
9. **Cue both decks at once**: no sudden jump in volume.
10. **Turn HEADPHONES MIX towards MASTER** with the level at 3/4: the master
    comes in at a sensible level, not a blast.
11. Cue a quiet track with the level at max: it is still loud enough.
12. On **Search**, type a query, then tap **Browse**: the whole folder is
    listed.
13. Tap **Search** again: the query is still in the box and the list is
    filtered again.
14. From Search, **LOAD** a track (you go back to Overview), then open
    **Browse**: the whole folder is listed.
15. Load a track that has hot cues: it loads at the first hot cue.
