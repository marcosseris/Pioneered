# v2.5.0-r41 — RMX: bigger roll buttons, Beat FX on the drums

<!-- debs: rebuild -->

New Mixxx build and new skin files. Install the update as usual.

- **Bigger ROLL buttons.** The roll rates (1 SHOT to 1/16) are now as big
  as the pads, instead of a thin strip. The pads got a bit smaller to make
  room. BUILD now sits in the pad row, the same size as the pads.

- **The BEAT FX buttons are the Beat FX again.** In r40, opening RMX turned
  the BEAT FX buttons into drum pads. They no longer do: the pads are
  played on the screen, where you can hold several at once. BEAT ◀ / ▶,
  FX SELECT, ON/OFF and LEVEL/DEPTH always work the Beat FX, with RMX open
  or closed.

- **Beat FX on the drums.** The RMX drums now always go through the Beat
  FX, whatever channel the CH SELECT switch is on (1, 2 or MASTER). Turn
  LEVEL/DEPTH and you get echo, delay or reverb on the drums as well. Echoes
  are in time with the drums, and the tail rings out after the last hit.

## Install (on the Pi)

```
sudo update-pioneered.sh
```

or Cog → **SOFTWARE UPDATE** → UPDATE, twice.

## Field checks

1. Tap **RMX**: the pad row (KICK, SNARE, CLAP, HAT, BUILD) and the ROLL
   row are about the same height, and every button is easy to hit.
2. **Hold two pads at once**: both roll. While a pad rolls, tap another
   rate: the roll changes speed.
3. With RMX open, press **BEAT ◀ / ▶** and **ON/OFF**: they pick and switch
   on the Beat FX, and play no drums.
4. Choose **ECHO**, switch it **ON** and turn **LEVEL/DEPTH** up, with CH
   SELECT on **1**. Hit a pad: the drum echoes in time. Do the same with CH
   SELECT on **2** and on **MASTER**: the drum echoes the same way each
   time, never twice as much.
5. Hit a pad and stop: the echo or reverb tail dies away by itself, with no
   sudden cut.
6. Switch the Beat FX **OFF**: the drums are dry again.
7. Turn the master down: the drums, with their effect, are still in the
   headphones.
